# Your Next Move AI

Build a premium, modern AI web/mobile app called My NextMove AI — an intelligent life-decision simulator designed for young people, with a strong South African-first focus.

Core Concept

The user brings their real-life problem or major decision. They should never be forced to choose from predefined problems. They can simply type something like:

“I want to move to Cape Town but I'm not sure I can afford it.”

“I got accepted at university but don't know if I can support myself.”

“I want to quit my job and start a business.”

“I want to change careers.”

The AI understands the situation, asks intelligent follow-up questions, learns the user's circumstances, and transforms the problem into a personalised decision simulation.

Core Experience

1. Tell Us Your Problem
A beautiful conversational interface where users describe their situation naturally.

2. Understand My Situation
AI collects relevant information such as income, savings, expenses, location, career, education, dependants, debt, goals and lifestyle.

3. Simulate My Options
Generate realistic scenarios and “What If?” simulations. Users can change variables such as income, rent, city, education costs, savings, employment and lifestyle.

4. Decision Analysis
For every scenario show:

Estimated income

Monthly expenses

Upfront costs

Savings requirements

Financial runway

Risks

Possible challenges

Opportunities

Short-term impact

Long-term potential

Survival Score

Life Decision Score

5. Compare Futures
Allow users to compare multiple possible paths side-by-side and understand the trade-offs.

6. Personalised NextMove Plan
After analysing the options, create a practical step-by-step plan showing what the user should do to improve their position and prepare for the decision.

Important Philosophy

My NextMove AI should not make life decisions for the user.

It should say:

“Here are your possible paths, what they could cost, the risks involved, and what you can do to prepare. The decision is yours.”

The AI should be honest, challenge unrealistic assumptions, clearly explain uncertainty, and never present predictions as guaranteed outcomes.

South African Intelligence

Design the product around real South African life. Incorporate relevant local factors such as:

South African cities and cost of living

Rent and accommodation

Transport

Food and utilities

Salaries and career opportunities

University and education costs

Student living

Business startup costs

Local economic conditions

Relevant South African financial realities

Visual Design

Create a beautiful, premium, minimal and modern interface.

Use:

Clean layouts

Elegant typography

Spacious cards

Subtle animations

Beautiful data visualisations

Clear progress indicators

Modern charts

Soft gradients

Professional dashboard design

Mobile-first responsive design

Dark and light mode

The experience should feel like a combination of a premium fintech app, modern AI assistant and futuristic life-planning platform.

The homepage should immediately communicate:

My NextMove AI

Bring us your problem. Simulate your future. Make your next move.

Primary CTA:

“Tell us what you're facing →”

Do not overwhelm users with complicated forms. Start with their story, then let the AI intelligently ask only the questions needed to understand and simulate their situation.

The overall product should feel trustworthy, intelligent, youthful, empowering and distinctly South African.
